# 137 - DNS Writes Scoped to One Workspace

## User Story

As a workspace owner whose workspace shares a Cloudflare zone with other workspaces, I want the DNS task of my workspace to change only my workspace's records so that no other tenant in the same zone can read a credential that rewrites my records.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] No workspace provision policy grants `read` on `platform/data/provision/cloudflare/<zone>`; before this requirement `roles/web-app-openbao/templates/workspace/provision_policy.hcl.j2` granted it to every workspace placed in that zone (finding of the [115](115-dns-record-for-bring-your-own-servers.md) review, operator pick 67fcb3a689820fb0). A unit test renders the policy for a workspace of the cloudflare DNS flavor and finds no such path. `test_no_provision_policy_of_a_cloudflare_workspace_names_the_zone_token` in `tests/unit/python/roles/web-app-openbao/test_zone_token_readers.py` renders every provision policy of two workspaces in one shared zone, with and without the store switch, and finds none; its control `test_control_the_former_grant_hands_every_workspace_of_the_zone_its_token` plants the former grant.
- [x] The zone token of `secrets.credentials.cloudflare_zone_tokens` stays readable by the platform identity only (`ansible-admin` or a dedicated broker identity), proven by a policy test per identity. `test_each_identity_reads_the_zone_token_only_as_the_platform` in `tests/unit/python/roles/web-app-openbao/test_zone_token_readers.py` renders every policy template and every workspace policy and finds the token read by `ansible-admin` and `dns-broker` alone; `test_the_broker_reads_the_zone_tokens_and_the_dns_scopes_alone` bounds the broker, and the controls `test_control_a_store_that_reads_the_provision_tree_is_found`, `test_control_a_workspace_policy_with_the_former_grant_is_found` and `test_control_a_missing_broker_policy_is_found` fail the check.
- [x] The DNS task of workspace ws-A creates, changes and deletes its own records in a zone it shares with ws-B. `test_an_own_create_is_forwarded_with_the_zone_token` and `test_an_own_record_is_changed_read_and_deleted` in `tests/unit/python/roles/web-app-semaphore/files/provision/dns/test_broker.py` forward each record call of ws-A in the zone it shares with ws-B to a fake Cloudflare with the zone token, and `test_only_the_record_module_points_its_provider_at_the_broker` there routes the DNS task's `cloudflare` provider through the broker.
- [x] The DNS task of ws-A cannot create, change or delete a record of ws-B in the same zone; the attempt is refused and the record of ws-B is unchanged. `test_a_record_of_another_workspace_in_the_shared_zone_is_refused`, `test_a_create_of_a_name_of_another_workspace_is_refused`, `test_a_rename_of_an_own_record_to_a_foreign_name_is_refused` and `test_the_workspace_is_the_one_of_the_token_never_of_the_request` in `tests/unit/python/roles/web-app-semaphore/files/provision/dns/test_broker.py` refuse each attempt with no call forwarded beyond the record lookup; the control `test_negative_control_a_broker_ignoring_the_token_changes_a_foreign_record` deletes the foreign record.
- [ ] A workspace of the knot and route53 DNS flavors keeps its current behaviour ([115](115-dns-record-for-bring-your-own-servers.md) acceptance criteria stay green).
- [x] The README of `web-app-semaphore` and `web-app-openbao` describe which identity holds the zone token and how a workspace's records reach Cloudflare. `roles/web-app-openbao/README.md` under `The DNS broker` names `ansible-admin` and `dns-broker` as its only readers, the `cloudflare` flavor of `roles/web-app-semaphore/README.md` leaves it with `dns-broker`, and both describe the broker's scope check of each record call.
